### Acceptance Commitment Therapy

Often called a rationalised form of buddhism and although this sounds a bit like a stretch, [parallels do exist](https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/abs/pii/S1077722902800414).

During ACT you go from recognising how you feel, to finding ways of working with yourself, to finding a direction, a broader principle in your life. One that is aligned with who you are.
