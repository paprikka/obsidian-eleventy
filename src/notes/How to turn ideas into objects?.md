***Get a grip!*** Shape an idea to see if you can connect it with others. 

Turn ideas into objects we can manipulate, connect, place in relation with each other. ([source](https://stephanango.com/evergreen-notes))

Some ways to get there:

- [Evergreen Notes](<../What are Evergreen Notes>)
- writing daily ([Learning through writing](<../Learning through writing>))
- building ([Make](<../Make>))
- talking and listening to people ([Say hi log](<../Say hi log>))


You can use the language as a chisel (or a grip), because [Language is knowledge](<../Language is knowledge>).

This reminds me of [knowing vs. "Knowing" through feeling vs. internalised knowledge](<../knowing vs. "Knowing" through feeling vs. internalised knowledge>) (internalised knowledge) in a sense that once we deeply understand something, we internalise it, and focus on the appropriate/needed/current level of detail.
