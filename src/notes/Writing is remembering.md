#fleeting 

Ironically I don’t remember the original note for this. 

Writing helps me remember:

- on a daily basis, it lets me both consciously commit certain events/patterns/emotions/… to my memory [Stream of Consciousness Morning Notes](<../Stream of Consciousness Morning Notes>) and remember moments that I had already forgotten for that day (weirdly enough, another way of remembering: [telling the story of your day backwards](<../telling the story of your day backwards>))
- during longer writing exercises I often recall a moment, a feeling, an idea about myself/perception of the world, an entire memory or a story/part of my life
- how much effort I put into my work. Often after finishing a project I just move on to the next one, without much reflection or appreciation (towards myself of the circumstances that allowed me to do it).
