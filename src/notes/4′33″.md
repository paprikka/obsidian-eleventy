A musical composition by John Cage. 4 minutes and 33 seconds of blissful silence.  

You can listen to it [here](about:blank). 

That said, fan-made versions and tributes can be found on the web, including [my favourite](https://sit.sonnet.io) where you, my beautiful listener, can choose the length of the piece yourself.

### Structure

4′33″ is divided into three parts:

- 30 seconds
- 2 minutes 23 seconds
- 1 minute 40 seconds

and, according to this [wikipedia article](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/4′33″) it can be played on any instrument!
