At the time of writing this I've had about 300 [Say Hi](<../Say Hi>) calls! Many of the people I meet share their art with me (digitally or *analog* via mail, if I'm lucky).  A recent caller made [made a graffiti with an anglerfish](<../Anglerfish by Lucas and Paulo>). I have a few anglerfishes in my notebook too, so I thought I'd share mine.

![Chthonic companion](https://images.ctfassets.net/hyylafu4fjks/7wd1FoLKsddppjxycuWB0A/1f7a8503e2036bd372b5b83b44be6264/226023183_2831673570419431_5892104880406410119_n_17925609661710057.jpg)

There are ~~three~~ four things I liked about this drawing.

**One, it looks like it was made by a kid.** It tickles my sense of perfectionism in a way that's a little bit uncomfortable. It also forces me to let things go: I can't fix or unmake it, you can't unsee it. My heart is lighter. [Thinking/writing](<../Writing is Thinking>) about this observation prompted me to write [Insomnia, Control](<../Insomnia, Control>).

**Two, the light from the lantern does not reveal anything and it doesn't cast any shadows.** It just removes the canvas, cue [Wittgenstein, Parmenides, or Parmesan](<../Wittgenstein, Parmenides, or Parmesan>) talking about death. (This post is titled *[Chthonic](<../Chthonic>) Companion*, what did you expect?)

**Three, the passengers are not travelling alone.** 

**Four, they're looking through the window and just admiring the reef (*through the window*, so neither back, nor forward).** The reef is only as permanent as their blinks and [saccadic intrusions](https://www.instagram.com/p/BRbsLEkjjtX/?next=%2F).  

This drawing is somewhat silly (but cheesy as it may sound) it left me a wiser person.
