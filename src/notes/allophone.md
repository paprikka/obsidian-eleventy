Variants of phonemes that carry no semantic distinction between each other called allophones. Compare ph in <u>p</u>in vs. s<u>p</u>in if you're an English speaker. 

An example in Polish: e in the final syllable (sień, cień) should sound flatter/wider than in words like pi<u>e</u>s
