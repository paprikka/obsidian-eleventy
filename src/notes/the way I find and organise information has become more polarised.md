I use DuckDuckGo or sites like marginalia.nu as dumb *text indexes* of the web, then LLMs (with or without web access) to *synthesise answers* or *solutions*. Google and friends sit somewhere in the middle, with a vague distinction between both approaches and being mediocre at either. 

When asking an AI a question, I have a stronger having of double checking the answer, because my bullshit detectors are set to *high*. When I need a response that's just *good enough*. When using an AI to answer a question, I'm more likely to follow-up by reading a paper or opening Wikipedia.
