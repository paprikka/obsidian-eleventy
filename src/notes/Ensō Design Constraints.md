### Longer version:

1. One click, and you should be ready to start writing.
	- That click should be on the app icon.
2. Private by default ([How I Use Analytics With My Indie Projects](<../How I Use Analytics With My Indie Projects>), [Defaults Matter, Don't Assume Consent](<../Defaults Matter, Don't Assume Consent>)).
3. UX stays out of the user's way, updates should make Ensō feel smaller/dumber ([MISS – Make It Stupid, Simple](<../MISS – Make It Stupid, Simple>)).
4. Encourage personalisation (related: [Reactive Hole](https://sonnet.io/posts/reactive-hole/)).
	- Avoid cognitive overload, analysis paralysis, or decision fatigue.
5. It should feel a bit like a toy (still looking for the right word here)
